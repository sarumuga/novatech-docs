NOVA TECH SOLUTIONS, INC.
Q4 2025 EARNINGS CALL TRANSCRIPT
DATE: JANUARY 28, 2026

Corporate Participants:
Sarah Jenkins - CEO
Mark Alvarez - CFO

Presentation Excerpt:
Sarah Jenkins: "Good afternoon, everyone. 2025 was a milestone year for Nova Tech. We successfully crossed the $125 million revenue mark and rolled out our next-generation enterprise suite. Our focus on operational efficiency expanded our margins by 200 basis points. Looking ahead to 2026, we are closely watching international macroeconomic headwinds, but we remain confident in our domestic growth pipeline."

Mark Alvarez: "Thank you, Sarah. Let me add color to the numbers. While subscription momentum was stellar, our professional services revenue did experience a minor 5% drag, hitting $15.0 million exactly, down from $15.8 million last year. This was entirely intentional as we offloaded low-margin consulting work to third-party integrators. Our R&D lift to $25.0 million represents our aggressive bet on AI primitives."

Questions and Answers Excerpt:
Analyst (Goldman Sachs): "Can you talk about cash positions and capital allocation for 2026?"
Mark Alvarez: "We finished the year strong with $45.2 million in cash. Our first priority is funding organic R&D. We are not actively looking at M&A right now, given valuation volatility, but we will protect our runway."
