NOVA TECH SOLUTIONS REPORTS RECORD FISCAL YEAR 2025 FINANCIAL RESULTS
Subscription Revenue Surges 22% Year-over-Year; Cash Position Strengthens to $45.2 Million

AUSTIN, Texas — January 28, 2026 — Nova Tech Solutions, Inc. today announced its financial results for the fourth quarter and fiscal year ended December 31, 2025.

Financial Highlights:
- Total Revenue: $125.0 million (15% growth YoY)
- Subscription Revenue: $110.0 million (22% growth YoY)
- Net Income: $15.5 million (up from $10.9 million)
- Year-End Cash Balance: $45.2 million

"Our financial results reflect the compounding value of our subscription model," said Sarah Jenkins, CEO of Nova Tech. "By shifting professional services to partners, we optimized our gross margin to 70%, giving us the financial flexibility to aggressively fund our enterprise R&D roadmap."

About Nova Tech Solutions:
Nova Tech Solutions, Inc. is a premier developer of cloud infrastructure software optimized for automated enterprise workflows.
