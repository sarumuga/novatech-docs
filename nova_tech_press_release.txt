NOVA TECH SOLUTIONS ANNOUNCES RECORD FISCAL YEAR 2025 FINANCIAL RESULTS

SAN FRANCISCO, CA -- Nova Tech Solutions, Inc. today reported financial results for its fiscal year ended December 31, 2025. 

KEY FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS:
* Total Revenue: $165.9 million for the year, representing a 25.0% year-over-year growth trajectory against the $132.72 million recorded in FY2024.
* Subscription Revenue: Achieved $142.50 million, signaling strong market validation of our enterprise cloud software packages.
* Professional Services Revenue: Reported at $23.40 million as the enterprise deliberately transitions implementation tasks to third-party delivery partners.
* Net Income: Surged to $11.2 million for the full year, a significant jump from $4.8 million in the prior year.
* Cash Position: Ended FY2025 with $45.20 million in cash and cash equivalents, providing a reliable operational buffer for subsequent market expansion.

"Our fiscal year 2025 performance illustrates the compounding scalability of our cloud model," stated Sarah Jenkins, CEO of Nova Tech Solutions. "Crossing the $165.9 million revenue milestone highlights the mission-critical reliance our customers place on Nova Tech platform automation.".
