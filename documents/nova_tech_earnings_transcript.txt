NOVA TECH SOLUTIONS, INC. FY2025 EARNINGS CALL TRANSCRIPT
DATE: JANUARY 28, 2026

Sarah Jenkins (CEO):
"Good afternoon, everyone. FY2025 was a defining year for Nova Tech Solutions. We cleared our revenue guidance targets to deliver a record $165.9 million in top-line total revenue, representing a strict 25.0% expansion year-over-year. This growth was structurally driven by our high-margin subscription layer, which pulled in $142.50 million on its own."

Mark Alvarez (CFO):
"Thank you, Sarah. To add specific context to the balance sheet and operational expenditures, we concluded the fiscal year with a healthy cash balance of $45.20 million. Our total net income reached $11.2 million, up from $4.8 million in FY2024. I want to address our intentional strategic pivot regarding Professional Services revenue, which came in at $23.40 million this year compared to $24.60 million last year. We are proactively migrating delivery dependencies to global system integrators. While this created temporary margin compression in our services delivery segment—hitting a gross profit margin of 14.0% as professional services COGS stood at $20.12 million—it unlocks massive operational leverage for our primary software infrastructure."

Michael Chang (Analyst - Vanguard Research):
"Thanks for taking the question. Mark, can you break down the professional services line? It dropped to $23.40 million, and the margin structure compressed. Should we anticipate this drag continuing into 2026?"

Mark Alvarez (CFO):
"Appreciate the question, Michael. The short answer is no. The $23.40 million revenue figure reflects the final tranches of legacy customization contracts we chose not to renew. As those obligations wind down, the associated $20.12 million services cost burden will drop off far faster than the corresponding revenues, clearing our path toward high-margin software predictability.".
