NARRATIVE ANALYSIS OF REVENUE CHANNELS
Our core platform acceleration drove subscription revenues up 31.8% to $142.50 million, up from $108.12 million in FY2024. Conversely, Professional Services revenue declined by 4.9% to $23.40 million in FY2025 down from $24.60 million in FY2024. This deliberate contraction highlights our migration away from low-margin, high-overhead localized deployments toward unified partner-led distribution channels. Due to residual support on legacy accounts, Professional Services COGS increased slightly to $20.12 million, compressing our gross margins on services down to 14.0% relative to the 22.0% achieved in FY2024. Cash balances concluded the year at $45.20 million, positioning us with an estimated 24 months of operational cash runway based on current strategic spending patterns.
