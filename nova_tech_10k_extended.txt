ITEM 7. MANAGEMENT'S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS OF FINANCIAL CONDITION AND RESULTS OF OPERATIONS (MD&A)

OVERVIEW
Nova Tech Solutions, Inc. ("Nova Tech" or the "Company") is a leading provider of enterprise AI-driven software-as-a-service (SaaS) architectures and cloud-native automation infrastructure. Our platform enables enterprise clients to deploy autonomous workflows, manage distributed model pipelines, and optimize operational telemetry. 

During the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025, we experienced robust momentum in our core subscription business, driven by a 42% year-over-year increase in Net Revenue Retention (NRR) among Fortune 500 clients. Conversely, Professional Services revenue declined deliberately by 5.3% as part of a multi-quarter strategic initiative to shift low-margin system integration consulting workflows to our global systems integrator ecosystem.

REVENUE METRICS & ANALYSIS
Our total revenues increased by $33,200 thousand, or 25.0%, for the year ended December 31, 2025, compared to the year ended December 31, 2024. 

* Subscription License & SaaS: Increased by $34,500 thousand, or 31.9%. Growth was primarily accelerated by the wide-scale market adoption of our "NovaNexus" multi-agent coordination orchestrator, released in Q1 2025. 
* Professional Services: Decreased by $1,300 thousand, or 5.3%. This intentional deceleration reflects our ongoing transition toward a pure-play recurring revenue software mix. Margins on professional services compressed from 22.0% in 2024 to 14.0% in 2025 due to transitionary severance and contractor exit expenses.

OPERATING EXPENSES & EFFICIENCIES
* Research & Development (R&D): Increased by 21.0% due to aggressive engineering headcount expansion in our foundational multi-modal research track.
* Sales & Marketing (S&M): Increased by 18.0%. S&M leverage improved by 170 basis points as digital self-serve pipelines and automated product-led growth (PLG) triggers deflected higher client acquisition acquisition costs.
* General & Administrative (G&A): Increased by 15.2% due to public company compliance, cybersecurity insurance premiums, and localized legal infrastructure expenses.

LIQUIDITY AND CAPITAL RESOURCES
As of December 31, 2025, our principal sources of liquidity were cash, cash equivalents, and short-term marketable securities totaling $45,200 thousand, compared to $31,450 thousand as of December 31, 2024. 

We believe that our existing cash balances, combined with cash generated from forward subscription operations, will be sufficient to satisfy our anticipated working capital, capital expenditure, and operational lease commitments for at least the next 24 months.
